# Build60 — 3-minute video script

About 430 spoken words, which is roughly 3 minutes at a relaxed pace. Read it once out loud with a timer and cut a demo step if you run long.

**Before recording**
- Record with Loom or OBS: screen plus a small webcam bubble.
- Use the **live site** (after deployment). Load it once so the 3D hero and fonts are cached.
- In `/admin`, keep the real date and WhatsApp group link set. Use a browser window around 1280 px wide; the 3D sphere only shows on desktop.
- Have open in tabs: the slide deck, the landing page, `/leaderboard`, `/kit`, `/admin` (logged in), and a `/proof/...` page if one exists.
- Zoom the browser to 110% so text reads well in the recording.

| Time | On screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:15** | Slide 1 | "NxtWave asked: get 500 final-year engineering students to register for a free workshop in seven days, on two thousand rupees. Here's my plan — and the growth engine I built to run it." |
